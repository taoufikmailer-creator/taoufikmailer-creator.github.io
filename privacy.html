Privacy Policy

Tangarrow game for Android · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what data the Tangarrow game (“the game”) uses, why, and what choices you have. The game is published by Finedum (“we”). In short: there is no account to create, we never ask for your name or email, we run no servers of our own, and we sell no data. The data that leaves your phone is processed by Google, for advertising and for Google Play Games.

1. Data kept on your phone

Your progress is stored only on your device: level reached, total score, stars and best score for each level, hints left, and the sound setting. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in the Android settings.

2. Google Play Games (leaderboard and cloud save)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses this Google service to:

  • show your Play Games gamer name and your rank on the global leaderboard;
  • submit your total score to the leaderboard, visible to other players according to your Play Games settings;
  • save your progress in the cloud (level, score, stars, best scores) so you can find it on another phone or after reinstalling.

This data is linked to your Google Play Games profile and processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage your profile visibility, or delete your game data, from the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings. If you are not signed in, these features are simply not used.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's Android advertising ID;
  • device information: model, Android version, language, mobile carrier;
  • your IP address, which may reveal an approximate location (country, city);
  • interactions with ads: impressions, clicks.

Google processes this data as an independent controller. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: on first launch, a Google consent form asks whether you agree to personalized ads. Without consent, non-personalized ads may be shown. You can change your choice at any time with the “Confidentialité des publicités” (ad privacy) link shown at the end of a level.
  • On any Android phone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone).

Rewarded ads (to unlock a level or get hints) are only shown when you choose to watch them.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: a short vibration when an arrow is blocked.
  • Internet access and advertising ID: added by Google services, for ads and Google Play Games.

The game does not access your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.

5. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell any data and share nothing ourselves. The only third-party services used are the Google services described above (AdMob, Google Play Games, Google Play).

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data about them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Retention and security

Local data stays on your phone until you uninstall the game. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies. Communication with Google services is encrypted (HTTPS).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, in particular under the GDPR, you may request access to, correction, deletion or portability of your data, and object to some processing. Because we keep no data on servers of our own, most of these requests are made directly with Google (see the links above) or by uninstalling the game. You can also write to us, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

9. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the game changes. The “last updated” date at the top of this page will change accordingly.
